I Chapter I

The Author

“Exploring the connections between history, culture, ancestry and human creativity.”

Born in Saskatchewan, Howard Brass has spent years following the threads that tie families to history: ancestral bloodlines, historical migrations, and the events that shaped generations across Europe and North America.

His research brings together genealogy, cultural history and documented historical events, and he shares it in his books and in articles published on Medium.

Alongside his writing he paints, self-taught and mostly in acrylics, guided by personal exploration and creative intuition.
