Howard Brass is a Saskatchewan-born author, historical researcher, and self-taught fine artist whose work bridges historical inquiry, ancestral heritage, and contemporary visual expression.

Part I

Author & Historical Researcher

Howard is the author of The Wheel of Life: Decoding the Holy Grail, a work that explores ancestral bloodlines, historical migrations, and the events that shaped generations of families across Europe and North America. His research focuses on genealogy, cultural history, and the connections between documented historical events and family lineage.

In addition to his book, Howard publishes articles and research pieces through Medium, where he expands on themes of ancestry, history, and cultural heritage.

Part II

Fine Artist

Alongside his literary work, Howard has established himself as a respected visual artist working primarily in acrylics. Entirely self-taught, he has developed a distinctive style guided by personal exploration and creative intuition.

His artwork has attracted collectors internationally and has been exhibited in both Canada and the United States. His paintings have been displayed at the Saskatchewan Legislative Building Gallery and were featured in The Auras of Brooklyn exhibition in New York City.
